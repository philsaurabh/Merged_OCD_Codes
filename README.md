# Video Obscenity Analysis
For Less than 2 minute video - 20 Frames per minute detection
For Less than 10 minute video - 15 Frames per minute detection
For Less than 30 minute video - 4 Frames per minute detection
For Less than 60 minute video - 2 Frames per minute detection
For More than 60 minute video - 1 Frames per minute detection
